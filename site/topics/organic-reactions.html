What it covers

Organic reactions is about turning one type of organic compound into another. You classify and write equations for substitution, addition and elimination reactions – for example adding hydrogen, water or a hydrogen halide across a double bond, swapping a halogen for an –OH group, or removing water from an alcohol – and state the conditions each needs. Combustion, esterification and the cracking of long-chain alkanes are also covered, often in a flow diagram that links several reactions.
