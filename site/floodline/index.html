When you need a flood line

Approvals that stop at the river bank

If your property is near a river, spruit or stream, the authority will usually ask where the 1:100 year flood reaches before it approves anything.

National Water Act, s144

Township establishment

No township may be established unless the layout plan shows the 1:100 year flood line.

Municipal stormwater by-laws

Building plans and SDPs

Municipalities restrict building in the floodplain and ask for flood lines on site development plans near watercourses.

GN 704, Regulation 4

Mining and industry

Residue deposits, dams and other facilities must stay outside the 1:50 or 1:100 year flood line or 100 m from a watercourse.

EIA and water use licences

Environmental applications

Specialist studies and water use licence applications use flood lines to set buffers and assess impacts.

Due diligence

Buying or insuring property

Know whether a stand or farm portion floods before you buy, develop or insure it.

Infrastructure

Bridges, culverts and drifts

Design floods, afflux and backwater, soffit and road freeboard, overtopping and culvert inlet or outlet control for new and existing crossings.

Method

Accepted South African practice, fully documented

Catchment from the terrain
Depression filling, D8 flow routing and watershed delineation give the area, longest watercourse and 10-85 slope.
Design floods — SANRAL Drainage Manual (2013)
Rational and Alternative Rational methods, the Standard Design Flood, a Regional Maximum Flood check and, where a DWS gauge exists, statistical analysis (LP3, GEV, LN).
Hydraulics — HEC-RAS standard step
One-dimensional steady flow through surveyed cross-sections, with conveyance, contraction and expansion losses and critical-depth checks.
Bridges, culverts and weirs
Bridges by the energy and Yarnell pier methods, pressure and road-overtopping flow; culverts by FHWA HDS-5 inlet and outlet control; weirs and drifts with submergence, and debris blockage where required.
Freeboard, sensitivity and rating
Optional freeboard and buffer lines, mixed sub- and supercritical flow, sensitivity to roughness and discharge, and a rating curve at the site.
Dynamic 2D check
The flood hydrograph is routed over the terrain with a local inertial shallow-water model to confirm the extent.
Mapping and CAD
Flood lines in Hartebeesthoek94 Lo coordinates, with DXF, KML and HEC-RAS files.

Questions

Flood lines explained

What is the 1:100 year flood line?

It is the edge of the area flooded by a flood that has a 1% chance of being equalled or exceeded in any year. It does not mean the flood happens once every hundred years; two can occur in the same decade.

Do I need a survey?

For the free check and for quoting we use satellite terrain. Approval flood lines must be based on a topographical survey or LiDAR, usually with 0.5 m contours. We can use your surveyor's data or arrange the survey for you.

How long does it take?

Usually five to ten working days after the survey data is available. Urgent work can be arranged.

Who signs the report?

The flood lines are certified by a professional engineer registered with the Engineering Council of South Africa (ECSA).

Will the municipality accept it?

The report follows the SANRAL Drainage Manual and HEC-RAS methods that South African municipalities and the Department of Water and Sanitation expect, and includes CAD files in the local coordinate system.

Can you include bridges and culverts?

Yes. Road bridges, culverts, low-water crossings and weirs on the river are modelled with their openings, piers, deck and road levels, so the flood lines include the backwater they cause. We also check existing crossings for capacity and size new culverts and bridges.

Does the free check replace a flood line?

No. It is an indication from coarse satellite terrain to help you decide whether you need a study. It cannot be submitted for approval.
