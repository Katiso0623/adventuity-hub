Flood risk checks before you buy or insure property

A property next to a river or spruit can lose much of its value if it floods or cannot be developed. Check it before you sign: start with our free online check, then get a certified flood line if the site is close to a watercourse.

Why check before you buy

  • Land inside the flood line usually cannot be built on.
  • Banks, insurers and buyers increasingly ask about flood risk.
  • Knowing the developable area early protects your price negotiation.

Questions

Flood lines explained

Is the free check enough for a purchase decision?

It is a quick indication. If the site is near a watercourse, a certified flood line study gives you the answer you can rely on.

How long does a certified study take?

Usually five to ten working days after the survey data is available.

What is the 1:100 year flood line?

It is the edge of the area flooded by a flood that has a 1% chance of being equalled or exceeded in any year. It does not mean the flood happens once every hundred years; two can occur in the same decade.

How long does a flood line study take?

Usually five to ten working days after the survey data is available. Urgent work can be arranged.

Who signs the report?

The flood lines are certified by a professional engineer registered with the Engineering Council of South Africa (ECSA).
