Become a tutor: teach Grade 12 Maths and Physical Sciences online

Adventuity runs live online lessons for Grade 12 learners across South Africa. We are looking for tutors who can explain Mathematics and Physical Sciences clearly, one-on-one or in small groups.

Apply nowHow it works

Who we’re looking for

Tutors who know Grade 12 (CAPS/NSC) Mathematics and/or Physical Sciences (Physics, Chemistry) well. If you teach Life Sciences or another subject, tell us in your application.

The work

Lessons are online – video, screen share and a whiteboard – one-on-one or in small groups. Afternoons and Saturdays are when learners book most. We discuss rates and hours at the interview.

What you need

  • A qualification or studies in a related field
  • A laptop and a stable internet connection
  • Clear explanations that a learner can follow step by step

How it works

  1. Apply with your CV

    Fill in the form below and upload your CV.

  2. We review your application

    Adventuity’s tutor reviews it, usually within a week.

  3. Interview and demo lesson

    A short online interview and a 10-minute demo lesson.

  4. Start teaching

    We agree on rates and hours, and you start teaching learners online.
