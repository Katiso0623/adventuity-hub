How booking works

From sign-up to your first lesson in five steps

  1. Create a free account

    Sign up in the portal with your name, e-mail and WhatsApp number. It takes a minute and costs nothing.

  2. Request a lesson

    Ask for a one-on-one time (60, 90 or 120 minutes) or a seat in a group class, and pick the topics you want to cover.

  3. The tutor confirms

    The tutor accepts your request or suggests another time. You get a notification and a confirmation in the portal, with the payment details.

  4. Join the lesson

    At lesson time tap “Join the lesson”. A private online classroom opens with video, screen share, a whiteboard and chat – in the browser, or the free Jitsi Meet app on a phone.

  5. Keep practising

    Between lessons, approved learners read the notes, worked examples with diagrams and step-by-step calculations, 325 exam-style questions with memos, quizzes, mock papers and a study plan.

Install it as an app. On Android open the portal in Chrome and choose Install app; on iPhone open it in Safari, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.

FAQ

Questions learners and parents ask

Is it aligned to CAPS and the NSC exams?

Yes. Every topic follows the CAPS Grade 12 curriculum and is grouped the way it is examined in the NSC papers: Mathematics Paper 1 and Paper 2, and Physical Sciences Paper 1 (Physics) and Paper 2 (Chemistry). Each topic shows its typical share of the 150 marks.

How do I pay for a lesson?

You pay only after the tutor has confirmed your booking, by EFT. The banking details and your payment reference are shown in the portal once your booking is accepted, and you upload your proof of payment there. The current fees are listed under Fees on this page and in the portal before you request a lesson.

What do I need for an online lesson?

A phone, tablet or computer with a camera and microphone (earphones help), a stable internet or mobile-data connection, and your calculator, textbook and paper. The classroom runs in the browser; on a phone you may be asked to install the free Jitsi Meet app the first time.

Can I book on my phone?

Yes. The portal works on any smartphone, and you can install it like an app: on Android open it in Chrome and choose Install app; on iPhone open it in Safari, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.

How long should I study each chapter?

Each subject page shows an estimate for every topic. For example, Differential Calculus takes about 8 h 20 min: 4 h 55 min of notes and worked examples, 3 h 15 min of exam-style questions at exam pace and a 10-minute quiz. All of Mathematics Paper 1 adds up to about 38 h 25 min, so spread the work over the weeks before the exam.

What if I need to cancel or move a lesson?

You can cancel a request in the portal any time before the lesson, and a confirmed lesson up to 24 hours before it starts. After that, message the tutor in the portal to move it. If you have already paid, the tutor arranges a new time with you.

Is the study material available without a lesson?

Study notes open once the tutor approves your account – usually with your first confirmed lesson. Before that you can already see every topic and how long it takes to study, here and in the portal.

Can I join a group class instead of a one-on-one lesson?

Yes. When the tutor schedules a group class on a topic, you can request a seat in the portal. A seat costs less than a one-on-one lesson; the price per seat is shown on each class.
