How the study time is estimated

  • Notes and worked examples: the estimate for each section, listed when you open a topic.
  • Exam-style questions: every question in the topic's practice bank at 1,2 minutes per mark. The Life Sciences papers allow 1 minute per mark (150 marks in 2½ hours) – the extra time is for checking your answers against the memo.
  • Quiz: 10 minutes per topic.
