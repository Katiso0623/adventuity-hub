Flood studies and flood lines in Mozambique

Mozambique sits downstream of large southern African rivers and is hit by tropical cyclones. Ports, mines, towns and roads on the floodplains need sound flood studies before construction.

Questions

Flood studies explained

Do you work in Mozambique?

Yes. We take on flood studies across Mozambique, including Maputo, Matola, Beira, Nampula, Tete, Quelimane. We start from your coordinates and survey, and arrange a local survey when one is needed.

How much does a flood study cost?

Every site is quoted individually. The price depends on the size of the catchment, the length of river, whether there are bridges or culverts, and whether a survey is needed. Request a quote for a fixed price.

What is the 1:100 year flood line?

It is the edge of the area flooded by a flood that has a 1% chance of being equalled or exceeded in any year. It does not mean the flood happens once every hundred years; two can occur in the same decade.

How long does a flood study take?

Usually five to ten working days after the survey data is available. Urgent work can be arranged.

Who signs the report?

A registered professional engineer certifies the flood lines, with the registration your approving authority asks for.

How do I pay?

You receive a fixed quotation. Once you accept it, you can pay online by card through a secure payment link.
